The user explicitly permits frontend-only local products, with cart and favorites saved in the browser. Arabic is the default (RTL), with a complete English (LTR) language toggle. The teacher needs a brand presentation at /brand.

**Why:** The user requested a polished school-project shopping experience, not a live commerce/payment service.

**How to apply:** Keep checkout and newsletter acknowledgements clearly identified as demonstrations. Do not add real payments, external order transmission, or accounts unless requested.

Outside the teacher's brand board, descriptive text must appear only in the current language, not Arabic and English simultaneously. Use «ترف» for TF in Arabic; English remains "Turf".

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected mixed-language descriptions and specified the Kuwaiti football term.

**How to apply:** Preserve bilingual brand names and technical surface codes, but render product descriptions and other translated copy in the selected language only. The brand board may show both.
